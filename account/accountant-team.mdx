When you join Rise, you're not just getting software — you get a dedicated team of certified accountants who monitor your books, handle anything the AI can't resolve automatically, and proactively flag issues before they become problems. Your accountant team works in the background every month so your finances stay accurate and compliant without you having to chase anything down.

## How the Team Model Works

Rise uses a **team model** rather than assigning you a single dedicated accountant. This means your account is always covered — no summer breaks, no sick days, no knowledge lost during handovers. Multiple certified professionals know your account and can step in at any time, giving you more consistent and resilient support than a one-person arrangement ever could.

## How to Reach Your Accountant Team

You can get in touch with your accountant team directly from within Rise, without needing to go through a support queue.

- **Chat:** Tap the chat icon in the Rise mobile app or web dashboard to message your accountant team directly. This is the fastest way to get a response.
- **Email:** You can also email your accountant team at the address shown in your **Account Settings** under *Accountant Team*.
- **Response times:** Routine questions are answered within **1 business day**. Urgent matters — such as an approaching tax deadline or an audit notice — are escalated and handled **same-day**.

<Tip>
  Use the chat feature to ask your accountant team anything financial — not just questions about your Rise account. They can advise on VAT, contractor vs. employee status, expense deductibility, and more.
</Tip>

## What Rise Accountants Are Not

Your Rise accountant team is here for your day-to-day financial management and compliance needs, but they are not a substitute for a corporate lawyer or specialist financial advisor when it comes to major transactions. For mergers, acquisitions, or complex corporate restructuring, your Rise team can refer you to specialized advisors with the right expertise for those situations.

## Credentials and Jurisdiction Coverage

All Rise accountants hold relevant professional certifications for the countries they serve — for example, **KLT** (Kirjanpitäjä, licensed bookkeeper) certification in Finland. Your account always includes at least one accountant who is certified for your jurisdiction, so you can be confident that your books are handled by someone who knows the local rules.
