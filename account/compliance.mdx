## Data Security

Your financial data is among the most sensitive information your business holds. Rise is designed with security at every layer.

- **EU data hosting:** All your financial data is stored in EU data centers, subject to full GDPR protections. Your data never leaves the European Union.
- **Tamper-proof archiving:** Financial documents are archived with cryptographic integrity verification — records cannot be altered after they are stored, providing a trustworthy audit trail.
- **Encrypted in transit and at rest:** All data is encrypted using industry-standard TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest, protecting your information at every point.
- **Read-only bank access:** Rise's open banking connection is read-only — it can see your transactions, but it can never initiate payments or transfer funds on your behalf.

## GDPR and Your Data Rights

As a Rise customer in the EU, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu) and the team will assist you promptly.

Your financial records are retained as required by applicable tax law — typically 7–10 years depending on your jurisdiction. This retention is a legal obligation, not a choice, and ensures your records are available if you ever need them for an audit or tax inquiry.

<Tip>
  If you receive a tax inquiry or audit notice, contact your Rise accountant team immediately. They will prepare and organize all required documentation on your behalf.
</Tip>
