The Rise mobile app is designed around a core principle — most days you shouldn't need to open it at all. When you do, it should take seconds. Everything Rise needs your input on surfaces as a simple notification and a one-tap action, so you stay in control of your finances without finances taking over your day.

## Home Screen Overview

When you open the app, the home screen gives you an instant read on where your business stands. You'll see your current account balance, any pending tasks that Rise needs your approval on, and a summary of your most recent transactions. If there's nothing that needs your attention, the home screen will reflect that too — no clutter, no noise.

## Notifications

Rise sends you a push notification whenever your input is needed — an approval request, a document to review, or a message from your accountant team. You can configure your notification preferences in **Settings → Notifications** to suit how you like to work. Rise will never notify you about routine bookkeeping it can handle automatically. Your phone stays quiet unless something genuinely requires your attention.

## Downloading the App

The Rise app is available on both iOS and Android.

- **iOS:** Download from the [App Store](https://apple.com/app-store). Search for **"Rise Financial"** or **"Rise Group"**.
- **Android:** Download from [Google Play](https://play.google.com). Search for **"Rise Financial"** or **"Rise Group"**.

<Note>
  The Rise web dashboard offers the same functionality as the mobile app. Use whichever you prefer — your data syncs instantly across all devices.
</Note>
