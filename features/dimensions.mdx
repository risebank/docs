Your chart of accounts tells you **what** you spent money on — software, rent, subcontracting. Dimensions tell you **what it was for**. Add a dimension like Project or Cost center, tag your invoice and expense lines with it, and Rise can split your whole profit and loss along that axis.

Dimensions are yours to define. Rise ships with none, so you create exactly the axes your business reports on — nothing more.

## Setting Up Your Dimensions

Dimensions live in your company settings in the Rise web app. Each dimension is an axis; each value is a choice on that axis.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Company Settings">
    Go to **Company settings** and open the **Dimensions** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Dimension">
    Give the axis a name — **Project**, **Cost center**, **Property**. Names are unique within your company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Values">
    Add the choices that belong to it: **Ratamo renovation**, **House 1**, **Jyväskylä**. Values only exist if you create them here — nobody can invent one while tagging an invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Default Allocation">
    Each value carries a default share, 100% unless you change it. When someone tags a line with that value, the share is pre-filled — handy when a cost center always takes, say, 60% of a recurring bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Note on When It Applies">
    Optionally describe when the value should be used — "all invoices from Kone Oy", "anything for the Ratamo site". These notes guide your accountant team today and will drive automatic tagging as it rolls out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Dimensions belong to one company. If your Rise account holds several companies, each keeps its own dimensions and values.
</Note>

## Tagging Invoice and Expense Lines

Tagging happens **per line**, not per document — one invoice can spread across several projects. Open the document, expand a line, and use its **Dimensions** block.

You can tag lines on:

- **Purchase invoices** — while approving, or afterwards as a correction
- **Sales invoices** — on saved drafts and on sent invoices
- **Expense claims** — done by your accountant or manager
- **Journal entries** — done by your accountant team

A line can carry values from several dimensions at once: the same subcontracting line can be both **Project · Ratamo** and **Cost center · Jyväskylä**.

<Note>
  Tagging becomes available once a document has been saved, because tags attach to saved lines. In a new invoice you haven't saved yet, the block tells you to save the draft first. Replacing the lines of a tagged draft clears its tags — Rise warns you before that save.
</Note>

### Splitting a Line Across Values

When one line serves two projects, split it. Enter either a **percentage** of the line or a **euro amount** up to the line's net value, and add a second value for the rest.

Rise does not force the shares to add up to 100%. Tag half a cost and the other half simply stays unallocated; tag more than the line and the report footnotes it as over-allocated. That flexibility is deliberate — partial tagging is a normal way to work.

<Tip>
  On expense claim lines, use percentages. Those lines don't carry a net amount of their own, so euro-amount splits aren't available there.
</Tip>

## Seeing the Numbers

Once lines are tagged, the numbers follow automatically — there is nothing to post, copy, or refresh. Correct a tag on an invoice and the reports change the moment you save.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="By Dimension Report" icon="chart-column">
    In **Reporting**, pick a dimension and a period to see costs, revenue, and net for every value on that axis. Defaults to your current fiscal year.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Unallocated Row" icon="circle-question">
    Every dimension view ends with what wasn't tagged in that dimension, so you always know how much of the picture is covered.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Single-Value P&L" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Run a profit and loss filtered to one value — the full statement for just that project or site.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Excel Export" icon="file-excel">
    Export any dimension view to **XLSX** — built for pasting into grant and funding claim templates.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Finding What You Missed

On the **General ledger** page you can filter to a single dimension value, or to cost and revenue rows carrying **no tag at all** in a chosen dimension. Click through from the unallocated row to see exactly which entries are still untagged, then tag them from there.

<Note>
  Because partial and overlapping tagging is allowed, dimension totals are an allocation view — they aren't meant to reconcile line-for-line with your official P&L. The report says so on screen.
</Note>

Employees never tag dimensions, not even on their own expense claims — they submit, and your accountant or manager tags. Auditors see everything and change nothing.

## Renaming and Archiving

Dimensions and values are never truly deleted, so your history stays intact.

- **Rename** a dimension or value at any time. Every past entry follows the new name.
- **Archive** a value you've stopped using. It disappears from the tagging pickers, while entries already tagged with it keep showing up in reports, marked as archived.
- **Restore** by creating a value with the same name again — Rise brings the archived one back with its history reconnected.

Every change to your dimension setup is written to the audit log, with the before and after values.

<Tip>
  Start with one dimension and a handful of values. A single well-tagged Cost center axis is worth more than four half-tagged ones, and you can always add another axis later.
</Tip>
