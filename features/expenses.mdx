Managing expenses in Rise means you never manually enter a receipt again. Rise pulls receipts from your email, lets you photograph them on mobile, and automatically reconciles every expense against your bank transactions. Whether you're filing a lunch receipt on the go or tracking monthly SaaS subscriptions, Rise keeps your expense records complete and accurate without adding work to your day.

## How to submit an expense

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the receipt">
    Take a photo with the Rise mobile app, or forward the receipt email to your company's Magic Box address. Either method works — use whichever is fastest in the moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rise extracts the details">
    Rise uses OCR to read the receipt and pull out the amount, date, vendor name, and VAT details automatically. You don't need to type anything in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic reconciliation">
    Rise matches the expense to the corresponding bank transaction in your account. The receipt is attached and the transaction is marked as reconciled — no further action needed from you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm if Rise is unsure">
    If Rise is uncertain about the expense category, it sends you a mobile notification. Tap once to confirm its suggestion, or select the correct category. That's all it takes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Receipt collection methods

Rise gives you three ways to get receipts into your books. Choose the one that fits how you work — or use all three.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile App">
    Open Rise and navigate to **Expenses**, then tap the camera icon. Photograph the receipt and Rise processes it immediately — extracting all the relevant details and queuing it for reconciliation against your bank feed. This method is ideal for in-person purchases where you have a paper receipt in hand.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email Forwarding">
    Forward any receipt email to your company's Magic Box address. It is built from your business ID with no hyphen or other punctuation — for example [**magicbox+1234567@risegroup.eu**](mailto:magicbox+1234567@risegroup.eu). Rise extracts the amount, date, vendor, and VAT information from the email automatically. This is the quickest option for online purchases where the receipt arrives in your inbox.

    The same address also accepts invoices, billing data and other documents. See [Magic Box](/features/magic-box).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Auto-capture">
    For recurring services — such as AWS, Stripe, or your other SaaS tools — Rise monitors your connected email inbox and imports receipts automatically without any action from you. Once auto-capture is set up for a vendor, every future receipt from that vendor is handled without you lifting a finger.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How expenses are coded

Rise uses a standard chart of accounts appropriate for your country, so transactions are coded correctly from day one. There are no separate expense categories to create or maintain — the chart of accounts is where bookings land.

If your business has its own tracking needs — per-project expense tracking, cost centres, properties — use **dimensions** for that. They are your own axes alongside the chart of accounts: the chart of accounts says where the money went, dimensions say what it went towards.

→ [Dimensions](/features/dimensions)

<Tip>
  Set up auto-capture for your major SaaS vendors once. After that, Rise handles those receipts forever with zero effort from you.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Keep original receipts for expenses over the VAT deduction threshold in your country. Rise archives a digital copy of every receipt, but some jurisdictions require physical originals for audit purposes. Your accountant team will advise you if this applies to your business.
</Warning>
