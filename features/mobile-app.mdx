The Rise mobile app is designed around a core principle — most days you shouldn't need to open it at all. When you do, it should take seconds. Everything Rise needs your input on surfaces as a simple notification and a one-tap action, so you stay in control of your finances without finances taking over your day.

## Home Screen Overview

When you open the app, the home screen gives you an instant read on where your business stands. You'll see your current account balance, any pending tasks that Rise needs your approval on, and a summary of your most recent transactions. If there's nothing that needs your attention, the home screen will reflect that too — no clutter, no noise.

## Notifications

Rise sends you a push notification whenever your input is needed — an approval request, a document to review, or a message from your accountant team. You can configure your notification preferences in **Settings → Notifications** to suit how you like to work. Rise will never notify you about routine bookkeeping it can handle automatically. Your phone stays quiet unless something genuinely requires your attention.

## Installing the App

Rise is currently in closed beta, so the iOS app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. An Android app is coming soon.

**What you'll need:** an iPhone, the email address you were invited to Rise with, and an Apple ID.

### Part 1 — Install Rise on your phone

You'll usually receive a direct download link from us. Open the link on your iPhone and follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install TestFlight">
    Install the **TestFlight** app from the App Store if you don't already have it. TestFlight is Apple's free app for installing beta versions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the download link on your iPhone">
    Open the download link on your iPhone: [https://testflight.apple.com/join/M9vCkdCY](https://testflight.apple.com/join/M9vCkdCY)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept and install">
    The link opens in TestFlight. Tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Rise from your home screen">
    Once installed, Rise appears on your home screen like any other app. Open it from there — you don't need to use TestFlight again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Part 2 — Sign in to your Rise account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation from your email">
    You'll receive an email invitation to join your company's Rise account. Open it and accept — you can set your password at the same time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the app">
    Open the Rise app on your phone and sign in with the same email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your company">
    You'll land directly in your company's view. If you have more than one company, you can switch between them inside the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If something isn't working

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The download link just spins and doesn't open">
    Try opening the link in a different browser or in private mode. Also make sure you're signed in to your Apple ID with the same email address the invitation was sent to.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You don't see your company after signing in">
    Your account has been created, but the company still needs to be linked to it. Let us know and we'll connect your login to the right company.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There's no code in the invitation">
    Open the download link directly from the email — the code isn't always shown separately; the link handles the redemption for you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  The Rise web dashboard offers the same functionality as the mobile app. Use whichever you prefer — your data syncs instantly across all devices.
</Note>
