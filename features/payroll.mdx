Rise manages payroll for your team, calculating gross-to-net salary, handling tax withholding, and filing regulatory reports with the relevant authorities. Your role is to approve the monthly payroll run; Rise and your accountant team handle the rest. Every payroll cycle is checked by a certified accountant before payments go out, so you can be confident the numbers are right and every filing is on time.

## The Monthly Payroll Process

Every month, payroll flows from the employee's phone through your approval and into paid salaries and filed reports. Here's how a typical cycle runs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Employees log their hours">
    At the end of the month, each employee opens the Rise mobile app and logs the hours they worked, along with any holidays or sick days taken during the period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manager approves the timesheets">
    The manager reviews the submitted hours, holidays, and sick days in the app and approves them. Any corrections happen at this step, before payroll is calculated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payroll is calculated">
    Rise calculates gross-to-net salary for each employee, applying tax withholding, employer contributions, and any adjustments from the approved timesheets. Your accountant team reviews the run before it's finalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payslips reach employees">
    Once payroll is approved, Rise initiates salary payments to employee bank accounts and makes payslips available directly in each employee's Rise mobile app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Rise Files Automatically

Rise takes care of every recurring payroll filing on your behalf. You don't need to log in to any government portal or prepare reports manually.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Income Tax Reports" icon="file-invoice">
    Employee tax withholding is reported to the tax authority each pay period, on time and in the correct format.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Employer Contributions" icon="building-columns">
    Pension, social security, and insurance contributions are calculated and filed based on the applicable rates for your employees.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Annual Payroll Summary" icon="calendar-check">
    Year-end employee income statements are prepared and submitted to the tax authority, with copies sent to each employee.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Payslips" icon="receipt">
    Professional payslips are generated for each employee after every payroll run and delivered automatically through the mobile app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Employee Self-Service

Employees access their payslips directly in the Rise mobile app after every payroll run. If an employee needs a copy of a historical payslip, for a mortgage application for example, they can download it from the app without needing to contact you.

<Note>
  Payroll regulations vary by country. Rise currently supports payroll for Finland-registered companies. If your company is registered in another Nordic country, contact your accountant team through the app for current availability.
</Note>

## Making Changes Mid-Month

If you need to correct a payroll entry or add a bonus after the payroll run has already been approved, contact your accountant team directly through the Rise app. They'll handle the adjustment and any re-filing required with the tax authority, with no action needed on your end beyond describing the change.

## Setting Up Payroll

Set up payroll once when you add your first employee. After that, the monthly process above runs on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payroll section">
    Go to **Payroll** in the Rise app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite employees">
    Invite each employee by entering their name, personal ID, bank account number, start date, and agreed gross salary. Each employee receives an invitation to download the Rise app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set payroll frequency">
    Choose how often you run payroll. Monthly is the standard in the Nordics, and Rise defaults to this automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first calculation">
    Rise calculates employer contributions, employee tax withholding, and net pay for each employee. Review the breakdown before approving your first run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
