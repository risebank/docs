Whether you need a quick pulse check or a detailed breakdown for a stakeholder meeting, the data is there the moment you need it.

## Accessing Reports

You can open your reports from either the Rise mobile app or the web dashboard at any time.

1. Navigate to the **Reports** section in the app or dashboard.
2. Select the report type you want to view.
3. Choose your date range — use a preset or set a custom range.
4. Export the report as a **PDF** or **Excel** file if you need a copy for your records or to share externally.

## Sharing Reports

You can share a report directly from Rise by generating a shareable link or by exporting it and sending it by email. This is especially useful for board meetings, investor updates, or bank loan applications — send a polished, up-to-date financial report in seconds without any manual formatting.

## Year-End Reporting

At the end of your financial year, your Rise accountant team prepares the official annual accounts on your behalf. You'll receive a notification when the draft is ready for your review and approval. Your accountants handle the heavy lifting; you simply review, ask any questions in the chat, and sign off.

## Custom Date Ranges

All reports support fully custom date ranges. You can view any period you like — last quarter, year-to-date, a specific calendar month, or a bespoke range that matches your reporting cycle. Simply set the start and end dates when you open any report.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the P&L report with a rolling 12-month view. Checking it weekly takes less than a minute and keeps you informed without any effort.
</Tip>
