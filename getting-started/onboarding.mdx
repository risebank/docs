This guide walks you through everything you need to get Rise up and running for your business. By the time you finish, your books will be live, your bank account will be connected, and Rise's AI will be actively categorising and reconciling your transactions — with a certified accountant team standing by to review your work.

<Note>
  Before you begin, have the following on hand: your **company registration number**, **VAT number**, and **business bank account details**. You'll need these during setup to ensure Rise is configured correctly for your country and tax situation.
</Note>

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [risegroup.eu](https://risegroup.eu) and click **Get started**. Enter your full name, work email address, and a secure password. You'll receive a confirmation email — click the link inside to verify your address and activate your account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your company profile">
    Once you're logged in, Rise will prompt you to complete your company profile. Fill in your **legal company name**, **registration number**, **country of incorporation**, **industry**, and **approximate monthly revenue**. Rise uses these details to apply the right accounting standards, tax rules, and chart of accounts for your region.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your VAT number">
    Enter your VAT number in the dedicated field. Rise uses this to automatically configure the correct VAT treatment for every transaction — so purchases, sales, and cross-border transactions are all handled accurately from day one, without any manual setup on your part.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your bank account">
    Link your business bank account so Rise can pull in your transactions automatically. This step uses secure open banking and takes just a few minutes.

    → [Connect your bank account](/getting-started/connect-bank)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your chart of accounts">
    Rise automatically generates a standard chart of accounts tailored to your country's accounting requirements. Take a moment to review the default categories. If your business has specific needs — for example, custom expense categories or project-based tracking — you can adjust or add categories at any time from **Settings → Chart of Accounts**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Meet your accountant team">
    Once your onboarding is complete, Rise assigns a dedicated team of certified accountants to your account. They'll review your setup, ensure everything is configured correctly, and send you an introductory message within **1 business day**. You can reach your accountant team at any time through the in-app messaging or by emailing [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

As soon as your bank is connected, Rise's AI begins processing your incoming transactions — categorising expenses, matching receipts, and flagging anything that needs your attention. You'll receive approval requests directly in the Rise mobile app for any transactions that require a decision.

Your accountant team will review your first month's books in full. If anything looks unusual or needs clarification, they'll reach out to you directly. After that first review, they continue to monitor your books on an ongoing basis, so you always have professional oversight running in the background.

<Tip>
  If you're switching from another accounting system, your Rise accountant team can help you migrate your historical data so you don't lose continuity. Reach out via the in-app chat or email [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu) to get started.
</Tip>
