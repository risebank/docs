Rise is an all-in-one financial management service built for Nordic SMEs. It combines AI that works around the clock, a mobile-first app that puts approvals in your pocket, and a team of certified accountants who handle what AI escalates — so you can run your business without ever getting tangled in financial admin.

## The problem Rise solves

Running a small or mid-sized business means wearing too many hats. Financial admin quietly piles up: receipts scattered across your wallet and inbox, invoices that need chasing, VAT deadlines that creep up without warning, and a back-and-forth with your accountant that drains hours you don't have. Most businesses cobble together a handful of disconnected tools — a bank, a spreadsheet, an invoicing app, a payroll service — and still end up doing manual work to hold it all together.

The result is what we call **management debt**: evenings spent reconciling transactions instead of planning your next quarter, mornings lost to email ping-pong with your accountant instead of leading your team. It's not that you're doing anything wrong — it's that the tools weren't designed to work together, and they certainly weren't designed around your time.

Rise was built to close that gap entirely.

## How Rise works

Rise follows a simple, three-step flow. Most of the time, you won't need to do anything at all.

<Steps>
  <Step title="AI handles it automatically">
    Rise connects to your business bank account via secure open banking — supporting 3,500+ banks across the Nordics and Europe. From the moment your account is linked, Rise's AI reads every transaction, categorizes it against the correct account code, matches it to invoices and receipts, and keeps your books up to date. It runs 24/7, with no breaks and no gaps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You confirm on mobile">
    When Rise needs your input — an ambiguous transaction, a new vendor, an expense that needs approval — you get a push notification. Open the Rise app, review the suggestion, and approve with a single swipe. Most business owners spend fewer than five minutes a week on financial admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Certified accountants handle the rest">
    Behind every Rise account is a team of certified accountants who review AI-processed entries, handle complex transactions, manage VAT and tax filings, and step in whenever a situation calls for human expertise. You get the speed of automation and the confidence of professional oversight — without hiring an in-house finance team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
