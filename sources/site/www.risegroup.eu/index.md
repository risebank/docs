## You didn't start a company to do accounting. So why is it eating your life?

Every small company pays a hidden tax — not in money, but in time, energy, and peace of mind. We call it management debt. And it's compounding every month you ignore it.

### Stolen evenings and weekends

Financial admin always lands when your real work is done. Expense reports at 10pm. VAT prep on Sunday. Board prep during holidays. This is not what you signed up for.

### The receipt graveyard

Receipts in pockets, confirmation emails buried in your inbox, PDFs hidden behind logins on Stripe and AWS. By the time someone asks, you've already forgotten.

### Email ping-pong with the accountant

13,000 emails a year and somewhere in there, your accountant is asking about a transaction from three months ago. You have no idea. You dig for 20 minutes. Every week.

### Fragmented everything

Bank in one place, invoicing in another, expenses somewhere else. Nothing talks to anything. You're the human integration layer between six different systems.

### The quiet anxiety

Are the books right? Would you survive an audit? You don't know — and that low-grade fear drains confidence from every business decision you make.
