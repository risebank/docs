## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How long does onboarding take?">
    Most customers have their account set up and bank connected within 30 minutes. The AI starts processing transactions immediately. Your accountant team introductory call is typically scheduled within 1–2 business days, so you'll have a real person familiar with your books right from the start.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to know anything about accounting?">
    No. Rise is designed for business owners, not accountants. The AI handles categorization automatically, and your certified accountant team handles anything that needs professional judgment. If you ever have a question about how something is categorized, just ask your accountant team directly in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch from my existing accountant or accounting software?">
    Yes. Your Rise accountant team can help you migrate historical data and transition smoothly from wherever you're coming from. Let them know what software or firm you're moving away from and they'll guide you through the process.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## AI and Bookkeeping

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How accurate is the AI?">
    The AI correctly categorizes over 95% of transactions without any input from you. The small number of cases that fall outside that threshold surface as notifications for your quick approval — usually a single tap to confirm or correct.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the AI makes a mistake?">
    Correct it with one tap. Rise learns from your correction for that vendor going forward, so the same mistake won't repeat. Your certified accountant team also reviews the books monthly to catch anything the AI may have missed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to reconcile my bank statements manually?">
    No. Rise reconciles your bank transactions automatically in real time. Your books are always reconciled to your bank balance — there's nothing for you to do manually.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Bank Connections

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Is my bank supported?">
    Rise supports 3,500+ banks across Europe via open banking (PSD2). Search for your bank directly in the app. If it isn't listed, you can upload bank statements manually or contact support to request that your bank be added.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it safe to connect my bank?">
    Yes. Rise uses read-only open banking (PSD2) — the same technology used by major financial apps across Europe. Rise can see your transactions but cannot move money or access your login credentials. Your bank account remains fully under your control.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my bank connection expire?">
    Banks require you to re-authorize third-party connections periodically — usually every 90 days — as a security measure. Rise will notify you when this is needed so you can re-authorize with a few taps and keep your transactions flowing without interruption.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
